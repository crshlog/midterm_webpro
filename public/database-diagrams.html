Freelance Job Board & Candidate Application Portal

Official Database Design (Conceptual & Physical Data Models) — EF234301 Web Programming Midterm

1. Conceptual Data Model (CDM) — Entity-Relationship Overview

A micro-recruitment hub connecting local businesses or student organizations with student freelancers (designers, coders, writers, tutors). Shows high-level business entities, business attributes, and cardinalities (1:N) without low-level foreign keys.

2. Physical Data Model (PDM) — Relational Table Schemas

Exact physical schema with primary keys (PK), foreign keys (FK), SQL data types, and cascade constraints matching the image requirements.
